# KKV/AI Candidate Filter - 2026-08-12

## Selected

### 1. AI-native finance team

Why it matters:
- KKV owners already feel finance pain: late invoices, unclear cash position, reactive decisions.
- The topic is concrete and close to money.
- It can become a service offer: "weekly cash-flow cockpit" or "invoice follow-up automation".

Suggested content angle:
- "Nem a könyvelőt váltja ki az AI, hanem a vakrepülést."

### 2. Agents need UI, approvals and undo

Why it matters:
- KKV leaders fear loss of control.
- This reframes agents as supervised operators, not autonomous black boxes.
- Fits Navibase/OpenClaw operating model: agent does the work, human approves key external actions.

Suggested content angle:
- "Az AI agent nem attól jó, hogy láthatatlan. Attól jó, hogy visszanézhető."

### 3. Readable AI output

Why it matters:
- Most AI output is too verbose or too abstract.
- KKV users need short, operational summaries and next actions.
- This is a simple implementation principle for client projects.

Suggested content angle:
- "Ha egy AI-válasz nem döntésképes, akkor nem üzleti output."

## Watchlist

- Meta Muse Glimmer: interesting for local-agent cost and privacy, but not yet a KKV-facing story.
- Computer-use agents: useful, but needs a concrete example from local work before publishing.
- Agent Plugins and Skills: important for productization, but too technical for a general KKV newsletter.
- GPT-5.6-Cyber: relevant for IT security providers, not general KKV audience today.

## Recommended Draft Topic

Use the "AI-native finance team" story and connect it with approvals/UI:

Small businesses do not need a magic AI CFO. They need a simple operating layer that watches invoices, cash-flow and follow-ups, then prepares decisions for the owner.
